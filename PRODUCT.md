# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
React 19, Vite, Motion.
## Users
French-speaking diners visiting a Portuguese restaurant in Nantes.
## Product Purpose
Discover dishes, consult the menu, reserve a table and locate the restaurant.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve existing French content, menu prices and contact information, confirmed by user. Add Google Maps. Responsive desktop and mobile. Private GPT Sites publication.
## Brand Commitments
La Cantine Portugaise. Rebuild the visual world with high quality photography and expressive motion, user requested. Independent unofficial concept.
## Evidence on Hand
src/data.js contains restaurant photos, menu and contact links. BRIEFING.md contains research. No invented reviews or awards.
